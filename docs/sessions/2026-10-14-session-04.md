Working, verified by data: the death flow from end to end (Space through the words, the page of numbers, both packs opened with Enter, back to the title); the map's opening line; shop rows and prices; the foresight choice; intents and statuses on enemies; the Item buffer on cards.

Problems found:

1. **Sounds stopped for good** after the death screen loaded: `[cue] disabled: ObjectCollectedException`. The generated clips belonged to no scene and the game's asset clean-up destroyed them.
2. **False "can't be played right now. Nothing here it can be used on."** after cards that had in fact been played (Toasty Kebab, Fiery Soup: Persistent cards stay in hand), on Unplayable cards (Sponge Shield), and with no reason on some plays (Flash twice, Whispered Secret, Stone pick).
3. **False "Combat started." and "You are in a fight."** while paying at the shop: the player model reports combat while the shop borrows the fight hand.
4. **No word on a purchase**: no payment progress, no "bought".
5. **Hits absorbed by armour said nothing** ("Played Stab." only).
6. **Death out of order**: "Health 0", then "Your turn", "Energy 3 of 3", five "is gone" lines, "Combat over", then "You have died."
7. **The start of every turn was cluttered**: "Energy 3 of 3. Your hand, 1 card. Roast. New turn. Your turn. Stab, 1 of 4. Drew 4 cards. Your hand, 4 cards. Stab, 1 of 4."
8. **An emptied hand** read "Your hand, 0 cards. Roast" and then an unasked "Enemies, 6. Lekker Limbs".
9. **Food aimed at a creature**: "This card acts on you" was wrong for food, and the arrow that landed on the player said nothing.
10. **The soup pot screen** read its heading and value but none of the soups, their costs or which were open; several arrow presses were silent.
11. **The whole Nearby list repeated** after a door was opened, and after a long time in any screen.
12. Smaller: "Soup Pot Encounter With UI" as a name; a card reached from Nearby read without "Your hand"; "Your hand, 1 card" read as the first card was dealt at a camp; "NEVERMIND" read while choosing a shop card.

## What we did

- **Sounds** (`Cue`): clips are marked to survive scene clean-up; a failure rebuilds them and only three in a row switch sounds off.
- **Card plays** (`CardPlay`): a play is counted by the game's own counters (`CardsPlayedThisEncounter`, `CardsPlayedThisTurn`), so a Persistent card reads "Played". An Unplayable card says "is never played: it works by itself while it is in your hand." A refused play says "was not played" with the game's reason, "It is not your turn yet", or "The game gives no reason"; the log now records the game's own `CanCast` answer.
- **Shop**: combat is ignored while a shop is open and for two seconds after (`Hud.InFight`). "Paid 1 of 3." for each food, "Bought Hot Poker." once the card has left the shop's offer. The passing "NEVERMIND" is not read. Walking blocked by a screen says "Finish with this screen first."
- **Armour**: "Weakling: 3 blocked by armour, no armour left." on the player's turn; "Takes 4, 2 blocked by armour, 3 left." when both happen; "Weakling gains 3 armour." on the enemy turn.
- **Death**: "You have died." is said at zero health; nothing from the fight follows it, and the scene change does not say it again.
- **Turn start**: hand lines are quiet through the enemy turn; the energy refill is folded into "Your turn. Energy 3 of 3."; "New turn" is not said in fights. Heard in a replay as: "Enemy turn. Weakling gains 3 armour. Lost 6 health. Your turn. Energy 3 of 3. Drew 4 cards. Your hand, 4 cards. Toasty, 1 of 4."
- **Empty hand**: the game's focus moves are not read; "Your hand is empty. Space ends the turn." once.
- **Food and other self-aimed cards**: "Aimed at you. Enter uses it on yourself; Left and Right look for another target; Backspace cancels." The player as a target is said as "You."; returning to a creature does not repeat "Choose a target".
- **Soup pot** (new `Soup.cs`): on opening, "Make a soup. Soup value 0. Watery Soup, needs 0, open. Spicy Soup, needs 1, locked. ..." with how to use it; "Soup value 1. Now open: Spicy Soup." as food goes in; each soup reads its cost and state; "Made Spicy Soup. It is in your hand." The game's own heading and value lines are not repeated. The first Up after food goes in is pulled back to the hand by the game as it deals again; the mod presses Up once more and hides the passing line.
- **Nearby**: a screen or a target choice does not make things count as gone, and a door streaming in the next tunnel does not wipe what has been seen.
- **Smaller**: joined-up internal names are cleaned after splitting ("Soup Pot"); a card in hand is the hand row even before the game's mode says so; the deal at a camp is quiet until "Drew N cards".
- **Help**: three lines (food targets, paying at a shop, the soup pot).

## Agile retro

- **Went well:** the log alone, with no marks, pointed at every problem; the key and speech timeline made a 28-minute playthrough readable in a few passes. Snapshots plus `addcard`, `energy` and `killall` reached the soup pot, the shop, a fight and a death within minutes each. The game's own fields (`_paidCostAmountInBowl`, `IsCardUnlocked`, `CardsPlayedThisEncounter`, `IsUnplayable`) answered questions that guessing from the screen could not.

- **Keep:** fix, replay on a snapshot, read the log, one item at a time.

## Risk analysis

- **The Up retry on the soup screen presses a key for the player** (L likelihood of harm / M impact): if the game ever pulls the focus back for another reason within 0.4 seconds of Up, the mod presses Up again. It is limited to one retry per press and to the soup screen, and logged as `[soup]`.
- **Hand lines quiet through the enemy turn** (M / M): if the game's phase sticks at EnemyTurn, the focused card is never read until an arrow is pressed. Arrows always speak.
- **Purchase detection relies on the card leaving `_cardChoices`** (L / L): a shop that keeps a bought card on offer would say nothing; the log records the check.
- **Armour lines depend on the turn phase** (M / L): armour that wears off during the player's turn would be reported as blocked.
- **The save:** dev replays loaded older snapshots, which also carry the game's unlock progress; the maintainer's save was restored afterwards from `after-playthrough-2026-10-12f`. Carried: everything in STATE's open items.

### What the maintainer asked for, and what was done

- **Paper Boy page** ("Discovered, Paper Boy, coming soon": suspected text bleed). It is the character page's labels: a count of starting cards found, the foresight heading, and a row of icons for a feature not yet in the game. Now said in words: the quote first, "Starting cards found so far: 2 of 4.", "Up from Let's go is the starting foresight, and Left from it the starting cards.", "A row of icons marked Coming soon: not in the game yet."
- **Mark 1, edge sound late.** The mod waited a quarter second to see whether an arrow moved the focus; now 0.12 seconds (the game moves it within about 0.06).
- **Mark 2, the backpack pile played the edge sound on every step.** The game's own focus does not move while the mod steps the pile; that is no longer taken for an edge. The pile's ends play it once.
- **Mark 3, "2 Terrys Touchy" ran together in the walking voice.** Every enemy gets its count ("2 Terrys, 1 Touchy"), and in the walking voice the names are separated by full stops, which it does pause on.
- **Mark 4, "Played Stab" is noise.** Gone; the game's sound says it. "Soup Pot placed, back right." stays.
- **Marks 5 and 8, the card landed on after a play, a draw or a new turn was read.** A new "silent landing" (`FocusProbe.Silent`): where the focus lands after a play, a deal, a new turn, a fight opening, a payment or a screen's introduction is noted but not read; the next arrow is read as usual. A turn is now "Your turn. Energy 3 of 3. Drew 4 cards." and nothing more.
- **Mark 6, the map's last line was read again at the end**, sounding like a wrap. At either end of any buffer the edge sound plays and nothing is re-read; the same at the first and last buffer.
- **Mark 7, the practice dummy was spammy.** Now "Practice Dummy placed, middle right." then "IF FATAL, INCREASE DAMAGE BY 2. Choose one of your cards with Left and Right; Enter picks it." The screen's heading, the separate text lines, "Drew 3 cards" and the card list are not read. On the panel: "Enter applies it to the card you chose; Backspace goes back".
- **Food in a shop.** The Player buffer (Control Right) starts with "Food to spend: N" while a shop is open; the shop's opening line says so.
- **Mark 9, choosing a card to buy was spammy.** Now one line: "Costs 2 food. Enter on a food card, then Enter on the bowl. Up is Nevermind." The food hand, Nevermind and the shopkeeper's request are not read. Cards swapped into the hand by a screen are not announced as "Added to hand".
- **Mark 10, "Combat started" on pausing and "Combat over" on unpausing mid-purchase.** The pause menu over a shop now counts as the shop.
- **Mark 11, paying repeated itself.** Enter on the bowl, then "Paid 1 of 2.", and back in the food hand silently.
- **Unpausing re-read the screen's introduction** (seen at every mark). Returning from the pause menu to the screen that was paused reads only the focused thing. The pause menu is recognised by its place under the pause canvas or its Unpause button, not by its kind: every menu here is the same kind, and the first attempt took the character page for a return from pause.
