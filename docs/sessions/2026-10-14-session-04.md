## What we did

- **Sounds** (`Cue`): clips are marked to survive scene clean-up; a failure rebuilds them and only three in a row switch sounds off.
- **Card plays** (`CardPlay`): a play is counted by the game's own counters (`CardsPlayedThisEncounter`, `CardsPlayedThisTurn`), so a Persistent card reads "Played". An Unplayable card says "is never played: it works by itself while it is in your hand." A refused play says "was not played" with the game's reason, "It is not your turn yet", or "The game gives no reason"; the log now records the game's own `CanCast` answer.
- **Shop**: combat is ignored while a shop is open and for two seconds after (`Hud.InFight`). "Paid 1 of 3." for each food, "Bought Hot Poker." once the card has left the shop's offer. The passing "NEVERMIND" is not read. Walking blocked by a screen says "Finish with this screen first."
- **Armour**: "Weakling: 3 blocked by armour, no armour left." on the player's turn; "Takes 4, 2 blocked by armour, 3 left." when both happen; "Weakling gains 3 armour." on the enemy turn.
- **Death**: "You have died." is said at zero health; nothing from the fight follows it, and the scene change does not say it again.
- **Turn start**: hand lines are quiet through the enemy turn; the energy refill is folded into "Your turn. Energy 3 of 3."; "New turn" is not said in fights. Heard in a replay as: "Enemy turn. Weakling gains 3 armour. Lost 6 health. Your turn. Energy 3 of 3. Drew 4 cards. Your hand, 4 cards. Toasty, 1 of 4."
- **Empty hand**: the game's focus moves are not read; "Your hand is empty. Space ends the turn." once.
- **Food and other self-aimed cards**: "Aimed at you. Enter uses it on yourself; Left and Right look for another target; Backspace cancels." The player as a target is said as "You."; returning to a creature does not repeat "Choose a target".
- **Soup pot** (new `Soup.cs`): on opening, "Make a soup. Soup value 0. Watery Soup, needs 0, open. Spicy Soup, needs 1, locked. ..." with how to use it; "Soup value 1. Now open: Spicy Soup." as food goes in; each soup reads its cost and state; "Made Spicy Soup. It is in your hand." The game's own heading and value lines are not repeated. The first Up after food goes in is pulled back to the hand by the game as it deals again; the mod presses Up once more and hides the passing line.
- **Nearby**: a screen or a target choice does not make things count as gone, and a door streaming in the next tunnel does not wipe what has been seen.
- **Smaller**: joined-up internal names are cleaned after splitting ("Soup Pot"); a card in hand is the hand row even before the game's mode says so; the deal at a camp is quiet until "Drew N cards".
- **Help**: three lines (food targets, paying at a shop, the soup pot).

## Part 9 — the strings table, in-game install, rebindable keys (same day)

The maintainer asked for the roadmap's remaining code items, and for updates to be downloaded and installed from inside the game.

- **Strings table** (`Loc.cs`): every phrase the mod speaks in its own words goes through `Loc.T("key", "English {0}", values)`; a `Mods\ShroomAccess\strings.txt` (`key = text`) replaces the English per key; a broken placeholder falls back to English. `tools/Export-Strings.ps1` writes `data/strings-template.txt` (248 keys), which the package ships; it fails on a key with two texts. The conversion of 14 files was done by a helper agent and checked by a replay of the camp, deck view, Bash, a fight turn and the buffers: the wording is unchanged. Left in English on purpose (the agent's report): the prefix checks that recognise lines for batching ("Nearby: ", "Your turn", the Status row's "Health"/"Energy" tips) and the English plurals of game names; a translation that changes those openings would lose batching, not speech.
- **In-game install** (`Installer.cs`): Ctrl+N, pressed twice within ten seconds, downloads the release's `ShroomAccess-*.zip` and its `.zip.sha256` from GitHub, refuses on a missing or wrong checksum, takes only `Mods/ShroomAccess.dll`, `Mods/ShroomAccess/...`, `UserLibs/prism.dll` and `UserLibs/prism-licenses/...`, renames each existing file to `.old` (a loaded DLL can be renamed though not overwritten), writes the new one, and on any failure puts the old files back. The next start deletes the mod's own `.old` files. Spoken: "Downloading...", then "installed. Restart the game to use it." or "The update was not installed: <reason>. Nothing was changed." `Package-Mod.ps1` writes the `.sha256` beside the zip; both must be attached to a release.
- **Rebindable keys:** `[ShroomAccessKeys]` in `MelonPreferences.cfg`, one entry per game action the mod answers, the four walk keys and the letters of the Ctrl commands (`CtrlPlusMark`, `CtrlPlusOpenUpdatePage`, `CtrlPlusInstallUpdate`); comma-separated Input System key names; an unknown name is logged and the default kept.
- **Verified by data:** the install step from a local zip with the game running (dev command `installzip`): 16 files written, the loaded `ShroomAccess.dll` and `prism.dll` renamed aside, the mod loading normally after a restart and the `.old` files removed; a mistyped key name ("rightArow") ignored with the default kept. **Unverified:** the download and checksum against a real GitHub release (none exists; the repository is private); Ctrl+N and Ctrl+O by key; a translation file; any rebinding pressed on a real keyboard; a game folder the user cannot write to (the install then says so and changes nothing, by design, untested).
