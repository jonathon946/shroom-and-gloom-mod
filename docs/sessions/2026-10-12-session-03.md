# Session 03 — 2026-10-12 — the seventh playthrough: grimoire silence, fight chatter, death screens

Scope: the maintainer played the Session 02 build (grimoire, a fight, then died) and reported four marks. Logs and marks are in `reference/playthrough-2026-10-12d/`.

## What the maintainer reported

- The Help buffer seems good. Statuses on the player read well (Seasoning, a foresight). Enemy status lines seem good.
- **Mark 1, grimoire:** Enter on a card was silent; Enter again changed the card; then nothing was readable with the arrows.
- **Mark 2, a fight starting:** the second voice rattled off nearby things while the first voice announced the fight, both at once. The maintainer's design: stop announcing nearby things while walking and play the sound only; at a camp, or when a fight starts, summarise; the two voices must never speak at the same time; the fight line is "combat started, the enemies, cards drawn, anything else there"; what was seen on the way into a fight is read after the fight.
- **Mark 3:** "will deals 3 damage".
- **Mark 4, death:** some text read, much probably not, and no way past the last screen.

## What was found

- **Grimoire.** The message is held back until the screen's text exists. Coming back from the pause menu re-armed that hold, the text was not found, and every focus line stayed held for eight seconds; the arrows pressed in that time said nothing. Separately, the result screen has only a Take button (the mod said "Left is No thanks"), so arrows there move nothing and were silent.
- **Fight start.** Arrivals were spoken through the walking voice as they came into reach, while the fight's lines went through the screen reader. Nothing stopped the two overlapping.
- **Intent sentences.** The game's tooltip is "DEALS 3 DAMAGE"; the mod put "Will" in front.
- **Death.** Three screens. The words spoken to the dead (`DeathText`) advance on Space with the real keyboard; they do not answer to any game action the mod can send. The page of numbers (`RoundUpUIManager`) has six numbers and a Continue button; the mod read four labels and no values. A finished quest then shows a sealed card pack (`CardUnlockPack`) that opens only on a mouse click, which is where the maintainer was stuck. Opened, it shows three cards and a "WOO!" button that the game does focus.

## What we did

- **Two voices take turns** (`Speaker`). A line the player asked for with a key is said at once and the walking voice gives way, saying its line again afterwards. A line nobody asked for waits until the walking voice has finished. The walking voice waits while the screen reader is, by the length of its last line, still speaking.
- **Arrivals.** While walking: the sound only, and the thing is remembered. Stopping at a camp: "You have stopped at a camp. Seen nearby: 2 Doors, Chest. Cards are dealt; Space ends the turn." Stopped by the game somewhere that is not a camp: "You have stopped. Seen nearby: ..." Standing still, a thing that appears is still said at once ("Nearby: Door"), since it is the result of something the player did.
- **Fights.** One line: "Combat started. Enemies: 4 Terrys, Lekker Limbs. Also here: The Encroaching Gloom. Drew 4 cards." The turn and energy lines at the start are dropped. The card under the cursor is read after it. Things seen on the way in are read after: "Combat over. Seen nearby: ..." A later arrival is "Joined the fight: ...", enemies only.
- **A hold never silences an arrow key.** An arrow that moves nothing plays the edge sound. The card-change message is given once per visit and waits at most 2.5 seconds for its text.
- **Card-change result:** "The card is now: ... Enter takes it." The message says "Up is No thanks", which is where it is.
- **Intents in the buffer:** "Will deal 3 damage", "Will shield self".
- **Death:** "You have died." on arrival; "Space continues." with the first words; the page of numbers read in full and kept in the Item buffer; "A sealed pack of cards. Enter opens it."; the cards in the pack read; the Continue button named.
- **Smaller:** pausing no longer makes the whole Nearby list repeat; casting a card no longer does either; the first-landing note is confined to the tutorial forest (it had fired at the start of every run).
- **Dev tools:** `die`, `roundup`, `pack`, `polled`; snapshot `after-death-pack-pending`.

## Confirmed by ear / verified by data / unverified

- **Confirmed by ear** (maintainer, this playthrough): the Help buffer; statuses in the Player buffer; enemy status explanations.
- **Verified by data** (replays, log): the grimoire from message to Take with no silent step; the fight opening line and the line after the fight; the camp line; the pack opened with Enter and its cards read; the page of numbers and its buffer; the edge sound on a dead arrow (title menu).
- **Unverified:**
  - Everything above by ear, above all whether the two voices now stay apart. The screen reader's speaking time is a guess from text length (0.4 seconds plus 45 milliseconds a character); at the maintainer's speech rate it may be too long or too short.
  - Space on the death words: seen twice in the maintainer's log, not reproducible in a replay. Whether Enter also works there is unknown.
  - What follows Continue on the page of numbers (the replay had skipped the death words, so it did not end normally).
  - The other two reward screens (a new path, a new character). They have an OK button the game focuses, so they should read as ordinary screens.
  - The fight line said by the screen reader after the walk key is released (in replays the key counts as held, so the walking voice said it).

## Agile retro

- **Went well:** the marks and the key log made each report traceable to a line in minutes. The game still running at the stuck screen let its UI tree be dumped before anything was changed. The game's own debug entry points (`CMDDie`, `ShowRoundUp`) reached the end-of-run screens without playing to a death.
- **Went badly:** a first-landing note added last session spoke at the start of every run; found only because a replay happened to start one. A `-Close` followed at once by a snapshot and a deploy failed because the game had not yet exited; the fix was to wait for the process. The room in the far-run save is rolled again on each load, so three replays aimed at the grimoire went to the wrong target.
- **Change:** in replays, find a target by stepping and reading the log, not by counting presses from an earlier run. After `-Close`, wait for the process to be gone.
- **Keep:** reproduce, fix, replay, one item at a time.

## Risk analysis

- **The voice arbitration is the riskiest change:** a line can now wait behind the walking voice. If the walking voice ever reports itself speaking when it is not, lines the player did not ask for would stay queued. Lines asked for with a key do not wait, so the game stays usable; the log would show `[queued]` lines with no `[say]`.
- **Less is spoken while walking.** A player who stops between camps hears the arrival sounds and must open the Surroundings buffer or press Up. This is the maintainer's design; the tutorial's first steps are the place it could confuse a new player.
- **The death words depend on a real Space press** the mod neither sends nor sees.
- **The pack is opened by calling the game's click handler.** A game update that renames `CardUnlockPack.OnPointerClick` would leave players stuck there again; the hook is looked up at run time and its failure is logged.

## WCAG 2.2 AA (spirit) audit — surfaces changed this session

- **Keyboard:** the card pack was reachable by mouse only; it now opens with Enter. No screen in the death flow needs a pointer.
- **Status messages:** a fight, a camp and a death are each announced once, in one voice at a time.
- **No silent keys:** an arrow that does nothing gives the edge sound; a hold no longer swallows an arrow press.
- **Timing:** nothing new is timed. The death words wait for the player.
